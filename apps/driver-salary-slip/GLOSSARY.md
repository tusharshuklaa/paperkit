# Driver Salary Slip

PaperKit's context for preparing a driver's salary receipt and the payer's reimbursement declaration.

## Language

**Driver**:
The person receiving payment for driving services.

**Payer**:
The person paying the driver and, where applicable, declaring that payment for reimbursement.
_Avoid_: Employer, employee name

**Salary Receipt**:
A document acknowledging payment to a driver for a salary month. It does not calculate payroll or establish tax eligibility.
_Avoid_: Payroll statement, tax certificate

**Salary Month**:
The month to which the driver's payment relates, distinct from the date payment was made.
_Avoid_: Payment date

**Payment Date**:
The date on which the payer paid the driver, which may fall outside the salary month.

**Declaration**:
The payer's editable statement accompanying the salary receipt, including any reimbursement request.
_Avoid_: Disclaimer

**Receipt Template**:
One of three arrangements of the same payment details, with different declaration and acknowledgement sections.

**Name Signature**:
A signature-style rendering of the driver's name, distinct from an uploaded signature image or an authenticated signature.
_Avoid_: Digital signature, verified signature

**Stamp Image**:
An optional image of a revenue stamp included in the receipt. Its presence does not establish legal validity.
_Avoid_: Verified stamp
