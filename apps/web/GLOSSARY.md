# Platform Shell

PaperKit's public introduction and discovery context for browser-based document generators.

## Language

**PaperKit**:
A free platform of browser-based document generators, including salary slips, fuel receipts, and rent receipts.

**Platform Shell**:
The public-facing introduction and catalog through which visitors discover PaperKit's document generators.
_Avoid_: Generator, document editor

**Catalog**:
The collection of PaperKit document generators presented for discovery.
_Avoid_: Registry, deployment

**Tool**:
A PaperKit document generator for a specific kind of paperwork, distinct from its catalog listing or sample document.
_Avoid_: Card, mockup

**Live**:
A tool that visitors can use through a verified, working generator destination.
_Avoid_: Listed, previewed

**Coming Soon**:
A cataloged tool that is not yet available for visitors to use.
_Avoid_: Live, operational
